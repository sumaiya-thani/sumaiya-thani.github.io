Shayki

An iOS app that gives women who recently started driving personalized oil change schedules and maintenance reminders.

Category
Productivity
Team
Team project, 5 learners
Platform
iOS, on the App Store
Integration
Siri

Overview

Shayki looks after car care for new drivers. It calculates personalized oil change dates and reminds you about the rest of your vehicle's upkeep, based on your driving patterns and your car's specifications.

Problem and objectives

Challenge: provide a solution for calculating personalized oil change dates and offering reminders for comprehensive car maintenance, to keep vehicles in good condition.

What it does

  • Vehicle profile. Engine cylinders, model, odometer reading, daily kilometers and oil type or brand.
  • Dashboard. Insurance days left, oil life, license expiration, car wash and regular upkeep.
  • Oil check by voice. Say “Oil Check in Shayki” to Siri and get your oil status without opening the app.
